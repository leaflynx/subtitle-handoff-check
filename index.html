    KEEP THE SCOPE CLEAR

    A technical check,
    not a final sign-off.

    This prototype does not compare subtitles with audio or video, certify translation quality, or guarantee universal delivery compliance. Automated text and timing checks can miss problems and flag intentional choices.

    Review findings against the actual destination specification. Required terms can appear in the wrong context; literal matching cannot judge meaning.
